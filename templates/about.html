ABOUT THE INITIATIVE

Technology that helps food find people.

FoodRescue AI is a student prototype for coordinating safe surplus-food recovery. It connects donors, NGOs and volunteers through a simple digital workflow.

01

Reduce avoidable waste

Use demand estimates and better donation coordination.

02

Support food recovery

Help verified organizations discover available donations.

03

Measure outcomes

Record accepted and delivered donations transparently.

Prototype limitations: This version does not verify NGO credentials, food safety, live GPS location, or actual delivery. Those require human verification, partnerships, security review and field testing. The project is not affiliated with FSSAI or any government body.
